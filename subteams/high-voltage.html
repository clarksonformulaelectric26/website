High Voltage

Battery pack design, power electronics, and everything that keeps the car's high-voltage systems safe.

The High Voltage subteam designs and builds the car's battery pack and how it interfaces with the rest of the vehicle — speccing current-carrying capacities, finding the right voltage-and-current ratios, and validating that the pack is safe to run. Every PCB in the system, from cell-monitoring boards to pack control electronics, is designed, assembled, and tested in house.
