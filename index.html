Our Car

The car above is the first one we have brought to Formula SAE Electric competition — the team's first complete electric car, and the one every subteam below has worked on.

It has not been retired. The car is now the team's testing platform: a vehicle we can instrument, run, and measure instead of a set of assumptions on paper. Thermals, pack behavior, how the chassis and suspension actually respond — every number that comes off it is insight we did not have the first time around, and it feeds directly into the design choices for the next car.
